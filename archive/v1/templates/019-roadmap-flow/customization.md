# Customization Guide: Roadmap Flow

The `019-roadmap-flow` template organizes your projects, repositories, and engineering deliverables into an open, transparent **Quarterly Roadmap & Horizon Matrix**.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Cadence Badges**:
   - Replace `Lucas Vance` with your name and role.
   - Update top badges with your current active sprint, release frequency (e.g. bi-weekly), and open RFC count.

2. **The 4-Column Roadmap Matrix**:
   - **Shipped**: List deliverables completed in recent quarters.
   - **In Flight**: Current active sprint commitments with target completion dates.
   - **Next**: Planned features that already have approved designs.
   - **Research / RFC**: Exploratory ideas under active investigation or prototype testing.

3. **Milestone Burndown & Open RFCs**:
   - Adjust the text-mode progress bars for each quarter.
   - Link real or proposed GitHub Discussions for open RFCs.

## Transparency & Accountability

- Demonstrates exceptional project management discipline alongside deep technical execution.
- Invites users and collaborators to participate in shaping your engineering roadmap.
