# Customization Guide: Mobile Developer

The `004-mobile-developer` template presents your profile as an authoritative **Dual-Platform Mobile Engineering Showcase**, highlighting native iOS and Android apps, store metrics, cold start benchmarks, and release pipelines.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Mobile Masthead & Store Metrics**:
   - Replace `Taylor Morgan` with your name.
   - Update top badges with your actual App Store / Google Play ratings, reviews, crash-free rates, and monthly active devices.

2. **Dual Device Showcase & Architecture**:
   - Update the ASCII mobile screen mockups for your iOS application (SwiftUI/TCA) and Android application (Jetpack Compose/MVI).
   - Customize the performance matrix comparing iOS and Android persistence, networking, and UI rendering stacks.

3. **CI/CD Pipeline & Store Links**:
   - Adjust the Fastlane automated release flow.
   - Update the TestFlight, Google Play, and contact links in the footer.
