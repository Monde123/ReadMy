## 📐 Studio Philosophy & Collaboration Model

```text
Discovery & Technical Spikes  ──▶  Interactive Prototype  ──▶  Production Hardening  ──▶  On-Site Deployment
   (2-Week Proof of Feasibility)      (Weekly Iteration Cycles)     (Cross-Platform Testing)    (Sensor Calibration)
```

1. **Prototypes Over Slides:** We do not pitch abstract conceptual decks. We build live, interactive WebGL prototypes that you can touch and manipulate in your browser on day five.
2. **Deterministic Stability:** Our gallery and public installations are engineered with watchdog timers and auto-healing services to run 365 days a year without human intervention.
