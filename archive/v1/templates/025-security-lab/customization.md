# Customization Guide: Security Lab

The `025-security-lab` template establishes your GitHub presence as a professional **Security Research Lab & Vulnerability Disclosure Station**, ideal for AppSec engineers, penetration testers, and cryptographers.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Lab Header & Credentials**:
   - Customize the lab name or your personal researcher pseudonym.
   - Adjust the 4 badges (CVEs disclosed, certifications like OSCP/CISSP, Bug Bounty rank, and Coordinated Disclosure badge).

2. **Threat Modeling & CVE Disclosures**:
   - Update the Threat Modeling table with attack vectors and defensive mitigations from your area of expertise.
   - Highlight 2 real or representative CVE discoveries / security advisories with CVSS scores and remediation steps.

3. **Toolchain & PGP Verification**:
   - Customize your fuzzing, reverse engineering, and SAST tooling in the matrix.
   - Replace the PGP key ID, fingerprint, and keyserver link with your real cryptographic keys.

## Security Etiquette

- Features an explicit Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure policy encouraging ethical reporting.
- High-contrast, clean GFM tables and monospace key blocks maintain professional authority.
