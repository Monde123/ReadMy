# ReadMy — Anonymization Policy

## Purpose

All examples in ReadMy are fictionalized. Source material may inspire a layout, section order, or visual pattern, but personal identity and identifying details must not be reproduced.

## Prohibited data

Do not include:

- real names or usernames;
- personal email addresses or phone numbers;
- private or precise locations;
- real employers, clients, schools, or organizations;
- personal social-media links;
- real certificates or credential identifiers;
- personal GitHub statistics;
- private project URLs;
- personal photographs or identifiable screenshots;
- API keys, tokens, secrets, or tracking identifiers.

## Approved placeholders

Use neutral values such as:

- `John Doe`, `Alex Martin`, `Jordan Smith`, or `Taylor Morgan`;
- `example-user`;
- `hello@example.com`;
- `example-project`;
- `https://example.com`;
- `Technology Company`;
- `University of Computer Science`;
- `Open to remote opportunities`.

## Fictional project names

Examples may use names such as `TaskFlow`, `DataForge`, `VisionLab`, `HealthConnect`, `LearnHub`, `CommerceCore`, `CloudBoard`, and `OpenNotes`.

## Statistics

Dynamic statistics must use `example-user`, a local placeholder, or a comment explaining where the reader should insert their own username. Do not publish the statistics of a real person in an anonymized example.

## Review checklist

Before merging a contribution, confirm that:

- no real identity remains;
- no personal URL or contact detail remains;
- project names and links are fictional or neutral;
- images do not contain identifiable information;
- dynamic services use placeholders;
- the content does not imply that a fictional profile is a real person.

> Preserve the design idea and the information architecture, never the identity or personal data.
