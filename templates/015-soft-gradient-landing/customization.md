# Customizing Soft Gradient Landing

This layout is meant for a friendly, approachable, product-facing identity.

Replace the headline, services, recent projects, and contact links with your real work. Keep the warm, soft visual style if you want a polished landing-page feel.

If you add illustrations or gradients, ensure they are subtle and do not reduce legibility. The template should remain readable in plain Markdown and in low-contrast environments.
