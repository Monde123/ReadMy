# Customization Guide: Academic Profile

The `011-academic-profile` template formats your GitHub profile as an authoritative **Scholarly Research Dossier & Bibliography**, suitable for professors, postdoctoral researchers, doctoral candidates, and research scientists.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Scholar Header & Metric Badges**:
   - Replace `Prof. Julian Moreau, Ph.D.` with your name and academic title.
   - Update your department and university affiliation.
   - Adjust the 4 badges (ORCID ID, Citations, h-index, and Open Access status).

2. **Theoretical Formulation & Publications Table**:
   - Include a mathematical formula, invariant statement, or theoretical model central to your work.
   - List your top peer-reviewed journal papers and conference proceedings with links to preprints (arXiv/PDF), reproducibility code repositories, and DOIs.

3. **Thesis & Academic Service**:
   - Customize the doctoral dissertation or thesis accordion with your abstract and defense artifacts.
   - List your Program Committee (PC) appointments, journal reviews, and advisees.

## Scholarly Credibility

- Built to mirror established scientific repositories (ACM, IEEE, USENIX).
- Provides instant access to open-source reproducibility artifacts alongside published papers.
