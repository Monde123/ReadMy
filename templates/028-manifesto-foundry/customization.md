# Customizing Manifesto Foundry

This template is ideal for founders, builders, and creators who want their profile to communicate a point of view as much as a skill set.

Replace the manifesto principles and the current focus with your own beliefs, products, and values. Use this format if you want to sound intentional and grounded rather than generic.

This profile works especially well for people whose work is shaped by a clear philosophy or long-term perspective.
