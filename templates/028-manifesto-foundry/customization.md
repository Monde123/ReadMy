# Customization Guide: Manifesto Foundry

The `028-manifesto-foundry` template formats your GitHub profile as an inspiring **Software Craftsmanship Manifesto & Architectural Charter**, ideal for senior engineers, software artisans, and technical purists who stand for engineering excellence over trendy hype.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Foundry Masthead & Badges**:
   - Customize the ASCII foundry header block.
   - Replace `Julian Vance` with your name and tagline.
   - Adjust top badges to emphasize zero bloat, durable code, and mechanical sympathy.

2. **The Six Tenets & Practical Commitments**:
   - Review and customize the 6 tenets to reflect your personal software philosophy.
   - Update the Rejections vs Commitments table with modern development practices you actively reject and the engineering standards you uphold.

3. **Shipped Artifacts & Seal of Craftsmanship**:
   - Feature 2 zero-dependency libraries, clean architectures, or tools that demonstrate your craftsmanship in action.
   - Update your repository, essays, and contact email in the ASCII seal footer.

## Architectural Purpose

- Establishes a memorable philosophical identity that resonates with top-tier engineers, founders, and teams prioritizing quality and longevity.
