# Selene Harrow

### Founder & systems thinker

I build the kinds of tools and experiences that make work more coherent, less noisy, and more human.

## Our principle

The best systems are not the ones that impress by complexity. They are the ones that help people move with clarity, trust, and purpose.

## What I value

- simplicity over spectacle
- durable systems over unstable shortcuts
- intentional design over reactive improvisation
- shared understanding over hidden complexity

## What I build

### The tools
I create interfaces, workflows, and product patterns that support calm, useful motion in real work.

### The culture
I care about the conditions that help teams make better decisions and feel more confident in their process.

## Current focus

- product direction for meaningful software
- sharp design systems
- better collaboration between humans and tools
