# Customization Guide: Robotics & STEM Engineer

The `008-robotics-stem` template treats your profile as an authoritative **Hardware & Robotics Specification Sheet**, tailored for embedded systems engineers, autonomous vehicle builders, and mechatronics specialists.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Lab Header & Badges**:
   - Replace `Dr. Liam O'Connor` with your name.
   - Update top badges with your robotics middleware (ROS2, ROS1), RTOS (FreeRTOS, Zephyr), and compute platform (Jetson, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone).

2. **Perception & Control Pipeline**:
   - Update the ASCII pipeline diagram with your actual sensor suite (LiDAR, stereo cameras, IMU) and controller nodes.
   - Adjust the Hardware Bill of Materials (BOM) table with your microcontrollers, actuators, and communication buses (CAN FD, SPI, I2C, EtherCAT).

3. **Robotics Platforms & Safety Compliance**:
   - Highlight 2 physical robotic vehicles, drones, manipulator arms, or open-source motor drivers.
   - Update the Safety Standards section with the regulatory frameworks relevant to your industry.
