# Customization Guide: Dungeon RPG Character Sheet (Template 033)

Designed for game developers, fantasy enthusiasts, DnD players, creative coders, and developers with a great sense of humor.

---

## 1. Quick Adjustments (5 Minutes)

1. **Character Class & Title**:
   - Choose your RPG class: *Full-Stack Sorcerer*, *DevOps Paladin*, *Frontend Bard*, *Security Rogue*, or *Data Alchemist*.
   - Update your Level (e.g. `LEVEL 28 JUNIOR WARRIOR` or `LEVEL 99 ARCH-LICH OF ARCHITECTURE`).
2. **Attributes & Modifiers**:
   - Tweak your STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA table. Write your own funny explanations for your strengths.
3. **Equipped Legendary Loot**:
   - In the Inventory table, invent items based on your daily gear (e.g. *Keychron Q1 of Clacking*, *Herman Miller Throne of Back Support +4*, *Noise-Cancelling Helm of Bose*).
4. **Quests as Projects**:
   - Turn your work/side-projects into "Main Quest" and "Side Quests" with quest objective checkmarks `[✓]` and `[▶]`.
