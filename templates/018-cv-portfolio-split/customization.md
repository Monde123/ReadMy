# Customization Guide: CV Portfolio Split

The `018-cv-portfolio-split` template uses an authentic **two-column split screen architecture** (35% Left Dossier / 65% Right Portfolio) via an HTML table layout that renders natively in GitHub.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Left Dossier Column (35% Width)**:
   - Replace `Julian Hart` with your name, location, and professional title.
   - Update your degrees, verified professional certifications (AWS, CKA, TOGAF), and core stack.
   - Add links to your downloadable PDF resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, and email address.

2. **Right Portfolio Column (65% Width)**:
   - Edit the Executive Mission statement explaining your architectural focus.
   - Feature 2 flagship production case studies with quantified business outcomes and technology badges.
   - Customize the Career Trajectory monospace tree with your company tenures and team leadership milestones.

3. **Footer & Styling**:
   - Customize or keep the confidential dossier disclaimer in the footer.

## Split-Screen Behavior

- The `<table>` with `width="35%"` and `width="65%"` ensures both columns align side-by-side on desktop screens while wrapping gracefully on mobile devices.
- High-contrast rules and typography guarantee crisp readability on dark and light GitHub themes.
