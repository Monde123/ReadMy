# Customization Guide: FinTech & SaaS Builder (Template 036)

This template is crafted for full-stack developers, SaaS founders, FinTech specialists, payment gateway engineers, and emerging market technology builders.

---

## 1. Quick Setup (5 Minutes)

1. **Header Badges & Typing Banner**:
   - Replace the links and labels in the badges (`GitHub`, `Portfolio`, `Email`) with your personal handles.
   - Adjust the lines in the `readme-typing-svg` URL:
     ```text
     https://readme-typing-svg.demolab.com?font=Fira+Code&weight=600&size=19&duration=2800&pause=900&color=0EA5E9&center=true&vCenter=true&width=850&lines=Your+Name+Here;Full-Stack+Engineer;React+%7C+Next.js+%7C+Node.js
     ```
2. **GitHub Username for Stats & Graphs**:
   - Replace all instances of `MathewTech254` with your own GitHub username across:
     - `github-readme-stats`
     - `streak-stats`
     - `github-readme-activity-graph`
     - `github-contribution-grid-snake`
3. **FinTech & SaaS Projects**:
   - Update the **Featured Commercial Products** section with your actual apps, dashboards, or SaaS projects.
   - Tailor the **Payment & Banking Infrastructure Matrix** to reflect the payment gateways you integrate (e.g. Stripe, LemonSqueezy, Paystack, PayPal, M-Pesa).
4. **Skill Icons**:
   - Customize the icons in `skillicons.dev` by adding or removing technology keys (e.g. `i=react,nextjs,typescript,golang,rust`).

---

## 2. GitHub Contribution Snake Setup

If you want the animated snake to crawl your real contribution graph:
1. Ensure your GitHub repo has a GitHub Action setup with `platane/snk`.
2. Ensure the output path matches `/<your-username>/<your-username>/output/github-contribution-grid-snake.svg`.
