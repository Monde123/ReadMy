# Customization Guide: Audio Synth Rack (Template 034)

Built specifically for audio DSP engineers, music tech builders, VST plugin authors, Creative Technologists, and hardware synthesizer enthusiasts.

---

## 1. Quick Customization (5 Minutes)

1. **Modular Header**:
   - Customize the patch cable connections in the ASCII box (e.g. adjust module names like *VCO 1*, *VCF*, *Wavetable*, *Delay*).
2. **Oscilloscope Waveform**:
   - Modify the ASCII waveform in the oscilloscope section to reflect a sine wave, saw wave, or square wave.
3. **Eurorack Modules as Projects**:
   - Fill the 4 modules in the Signal Chain table with your own audio effects, DSP libraries, synthesizers, or sound design research.
4. **Signal Flow Schematic**:
   - Replace the ASCII signal pipeline diagram with your own processing chain (e.g. `Mic In -> Compressor -> EQ -> Master Out`).
