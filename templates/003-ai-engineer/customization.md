# Customization Guide: AI Engineer

The `003-ai-engineer` template presents your profile as an authoritative **AI & Machine Learning Systems Dashboard**, highlighting model registries, inference latency, benchmark scores, and GPU infrastructure.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Hardware Badges**:
   - Replace `Dr. Elena Rostova` with your name.
   - Adjust your GPU compute badge (e.g. `8x H100 SXM5`, `4x A100 80GB`, `Apple M3 Max`) and HuggingFace handle.

2. **Model Registry & Inference Flow**:
   - Update the Model Registry table with your trained models, weights, fine-tunes, or parameter sizes.
   - Modify the ASCII inference pipeline to reflect your actual architecture (e.g. guardrails, vector database, re-ranker, inference engine).

3. **Open Weights & MLOps Toolchain**:
   - Feature 2 flagship ML models or open-source inference libraries with download counters or licenses.
   - Update the MLOps infrastructure table with your frameworks (PyTorch, DeepSpeed, Triton, etc.).

## Theme Resilience

- All tables and diagrams are formatted in pure GFM and ASCII, providing instant legibility on mobile, light mode, and dark mode without broken SVG artifacts.
