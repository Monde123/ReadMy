# Customization Guide: The Quantum Prism // Tri-Cascade (Template 040)

An avant-garde, geometric, and concept-driven template designed for researchers, creative technologists, shader magicians, and frontier builders who refuse conventional GitHub templates.

---

## 1. The Tri-Cascade Concept

This profile is structured like a prism:
1. **The Monad (Apex)**: The single philosophical drive that motivates all your creations.
2. **The Tri-Vertex**:
   - **Vertex I (Theoretical Void)**: Deep algorithms, cryptographic proofs, zero-knowledge, or pure systems math.
   - **Vertex II (Neural Forge)**: Autonomous agents, LLM pipelines, synthetic cognition, neural networks.
   - **Vertex III (Kinetic Realm)**: Shaders, WebGPU, spatial audio, generative visual arts, game mechanics.
3. **The Spectral Diffraction Matrix**: Group your skills not by job titles, but by energy levels (Ultraviolet, Visible Light, Deep Infrared).
4. **The Inverse Pyramid**: Cascading project list with narrowing visual width.

---

## 2. Quick Customization (5 Minutes)

1. **Header Typography**:
   - The typing banner uses the serif classical font `Cinzel` with an amber/gold tone (`E0A96D`).
   - Adjust the lines in the `readme-typing-svg` URL to reflect your singular mission.
2. **Tri-Vertex Content**:
   - Fill in your actual projects across the three vertices. If you are not into shaders or ZK, you can redefine the vertices (e.g. *Vertex I: Cloud Architecture, Vertex II: Product Design, Vertex III: Hardware IoT*).
3. **Inverse Pyramid Cascade**:
   - Update `[PRISM-01]` through `[PRISM-06]` with your flagship repos.
4. **GitHub Analytics**:
   - Replace `octocat` with your GitHub username in the radical-themed streak stats and language distribution.
