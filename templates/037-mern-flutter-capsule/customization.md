# Customization Guide: MERN & Flutter Capsule (Template 037)

Tailored for Full-Stack developers (MERN stack, Next.js) and cross-platform mobile engineers (Flutter, React Native) who want a dynamic, capsule-styled GitHub README.

---

## 1. Quick Customization (5 Minutes)

1. **Capsule Render Header & Footer**:
   - Customize the text, gradient colors, and font size in the `capsule-render` URL:
     ```text
     https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=waving&color=gradient&customColorList=6,11,20&height=200&section=header&text=Your+Name&fontSize=42
     ```
   - Color palettes: change `customColorList=6,11,20` (or try `0,2,8`, `12,24,30`).
2. **Spotify Recently Played**:
   - In the Spotify widget URL:
     ```text
     https://spotify-recently-played-readme.vercel.app/api?user=YOUR_SPOTIFY_USER_ID&count=4&unique=true
     ```
   - Or replace with your favorite music platform if you prefer.
3. **Animated Typing SVG**:
   - Modify the lines in the `readme-typing-svg` URL to state your main title and target roles.
4. **GitHub Analytics**:
   - Replace `octocat` with your GitHub username across:
     - `github-readme-stats`
     - `streak-stats`
     - `github-profile-trophy`
     - `github-contribution-grid-snake`
5. **Interactive Project Accordions**:
   - Expand or add `<details><summary>` blocks with your own apps, screenshots, and live demo URLs.
