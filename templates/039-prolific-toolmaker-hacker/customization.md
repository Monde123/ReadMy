# Customization Guide: Prolific Toolmaker & CLI Crafter (Template 039)

Designed for developer tool creators, npm package authors, CLI engineers, Rust/Go toolmakers, and makers with dozens of projects.

---

## 1. Quick Customization (5 Minutes)

1. **Header & Contact Badges**:
   - Replace the personal website, email, and npm profile links at the top.
2. **Icons via `go-skill-icons`**:
   - Adjust the comma-separated `i=` parameter in the URLs:
     - Languages: `i=javascript,typescript,golang,python,bash,rust`
     - Backend: `i=nodejs,expressjs,nestjs,postgresql,mongodb,supabase`
     - Desktop/Mobile: `i=tauri,electron,flutter,reactnative`
3. **Structured Project Catalog**:
   - **Active Products**: List 4-6 of your main apps or active web services with brief elevator pitches.
   - **Developer Utilities & CLI Packages**: List your npm libraries, GitHub CLI tools, or browser extensions.
   - **Legacy Archives**: Keep a historical section of past experiments, hacks, and algorithms.
4. **BuyMeACoffee Button**:
   - Replace the link `https://example.com` with your real `buymeacoffee.com/<username>` link.
5. **Stats & Streak**:
   - Replace `octocat` with your GitHub handle.
