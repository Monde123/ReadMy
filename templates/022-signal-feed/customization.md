# Customization Guide: Signal Feed

The `022-signal-feed` template formats your GitHub profile as an authentic **Technical Signal Dispatch & Reading Log**, ideal for engineering bloggers, technology writers, research curators, and systems commentators.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Publication Metrics**:
   - Customize the ASCII radio station header block with your newsletter or publication title.
   - Replace `Clara Valmont` with your name.
   - Update top badges with your dispatch count, subscriber count, and RSS feed format.

2. **Recent Dispatches & Annotated Papers**:
   - Fill out the Recent Dispatches table with links to your latest technical posts, articles, or newsletter issues (including date, category, and read time).
   - Add notes or key takeaways from papers and books you are currently reading.

3. **Technology Radar & Subscription Options**:
   - Populate your personal Tech Radar with technologies categorized under `ADOPT`, `TRIAL`, `ASSESS`, and `HOLD`.
   - Update the footer with your RSS feed URL, newsletter signup, and social links.

## Intellectual Depth

- Signals that you read deeply, synthesize complex information, and stay ahead of technical trends without chasing shallow hype cycles.
