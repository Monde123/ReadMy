# Customizing Signal Feed

This template is designed for profiles that feel more like a public notebook or a living stream of insight than a static résumé.

Replace the signal entries, reading list, and community notes with your own topics. Keep the feed rhythm if it suits your voice, or simplify it into a few well-structured sections.

The feed approach works especially well for technical communicators, educators, community leaders, and maintainers who want a more dynamic presence.
