# Customizing Documentary Profile

Use this template when your work benefits from a narrative arc, a chaptered structure, or a strong story-driven personal brand.

Replace the chapter titles and thematic descriptions with your own path, milestones, and focus areas. Keep the narrative tone if it fits your profile, or adjust to a more direct or technical voice.

This template is especially suitable for builders who want to present their work as a progression of decisions, learning, and public impact.
