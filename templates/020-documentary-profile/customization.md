# Customization Guide: Documentary Profile

The `020-documentary-profile` template formats your GitHub presence as an authoritative **Systems Forensics & Long-Form Case Study Archive**, designed for Principal Investigators, SRE Directors, and Systems Architects who write deep technical post-mortems.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Investigative Metrics**:
   - Customize the ASCII station header block.
   - Replace `Dr. Henrik Lindqvist` with your name.
   - Update badges with your number of published case studies, incidents deconstructed, and disciplinary focus.

2. **Featured Documentary Case Study**:
   - Write out an authentic technical investigation using the 4-part structure:
     1. The Initial Anomaly (timestamp, trigger).
     2. The Cascade Dynamics (how the failure propagated across subsystems).
     3. The Forensic Discovery & Root Cause (the hidden race condition or bug).
     4. The Architectural Remediation (protocols fixed, alerts added, code rewritten).

3. **Case Archive & Systems Philosophy**:
   - List prior cases in the forensic archive table with scale and incident classification.
   - Personalize your systems engineering philosophy and links to full PDF reports or TLA+ specs.

## Investigative Authority

- Communicates deep engineering maturity by focusing on how complex distributed systems actually behave under stress.
- Highly attractive to engineering executives seeking senior talent capable of preventing catastrophic downtime.
