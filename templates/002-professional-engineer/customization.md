# Customization Guide: Professional Engineer (RFC Format)

The `002-professional-engineer` template frames your profile README as a formal IETF RFC document / technical engineering datasheet, signaling deep systems rigour and enterprise backend leadership.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **RFC Header & Abstract**:
   - Replace `A. VANCE` and `Alex Vance` with your name and credentials.
   - Adjust RFC number, date, and abstract statement describing your core engineering focus.

2. **System Parameters & Normative Tenets**:
   - Customize the System Parameters table with your runtimes, databases, message brokers, and uptime targets.
   - Edit the 4 normative tenets (`MUST`, `SHOULD`, `MUST NOT`) to reflect your engineering philosophy.

3. **Reference Implementations & Colophon**:
   - Describe 2 production systems as RFC references with throughput, correctness verification, and stack badges.
   - Replace the GPG key fingerprint, email, and website links in the colophon.
