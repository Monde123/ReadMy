# Customization Guide: Deep Space Astrophysics (Template 032)

Built specifically for aerospace engineers, scientific computing specialists, astrophysics researchers, and simulation programmers.

## 1. Quick Adjustments (5 Minutes)

1. **Spacecraft & Call Sign**:
   - Change `VOYAGER-IX` to your favorite probe, spacecraft, or personal project codename (e.g. `ARTEMIS-LAB`, `NEW-HORIZONS-II`, `KEPLER-ORBITER`).
   - Customize the ASCII rocket/satellite in the header banner.
2. **Telemetry Console**:
   - Adjust the scientific metrics (velocity, AU distance from Earth, signal delay) or replace them with your engineering stats (e.g., lines of code written, supercomputing cluster hours, papers published).
3. **Instruments as Projects**:
   - Map your real data pipelines, ML models, or open-source packages to the 4 Payload Instrumentation slots (MAG, CRS, PWS, etc.).
