# Customization Guide: Mosaic Portfolio

The `021-mosaic-portfolio` template arranges your projects in an asymmetric **mosaic exhibition grid**, alternating between wide highlight cards (65% width) and companion cards (35% width) to create visual rhythm.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Archival Badge Bar**:
   - Customize the ASCII exhibition header box with your name, studio name, and city nodes.
   - Adjust the 4 monochrome discipline badges (e.g. `Creative Technology`, `Interactive Web`, etc.).

2. **Project Mosaic Matrix**:
   - **Row 1**: Hero Project (65% width) paired with a Compact Study (35% width).
   - **Row 2**: Compact Study (35% width) paired with a Deep Exhibition Project (65% width).
   - Replace project titles, descriptions, technical stack tags, and exhibition links.

3. **Material Specifications & Curatorial Stance**:
   - Update the Material Specifications table with your creative computation primitives (shaders, graphics, audio, physical hardware).
   - Edit the Curatorial Stance quote with your design philosophy.

## Layout Technique

- Achieved through alternating `colspan` and percentage widths (`width="65%"` and `width="35%"`) inside a standard HTML table.
- Monochromatic badge palette (`#111111`, `#333333`, `#555555`, `#777777`) offers high editorial sophistication.
