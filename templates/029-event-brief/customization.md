# Customization Guide: Event Brief

The `029-event-brief` template formats your GitHub profile as an authoritative **Keynote Speaker One-Sheet & Technical Workshop Dossier**, designed for conference speakers, keynote presenters, and corporate trainers.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Speaker Masthead & Snapshot Badges**:
   - Customize the station header block with your target conference years.
   - Replace `Dr. Simone Dubois` with your name and keynote focus.
   - Update badges with your keynotes delivered, total attendee reach, average speaker rating, and booking availability.

2. **Featured Talks & Workshop Catalog**:
   - Feature 2 flagship keynotes with abstracts, target audience tags, and key audience takeaways.
   - List your half-day or full-day hands-on technical workshops with prerequisite lab exercises.

3. **Speaker Rider & Testimonials**:
   - Customize your A/V requirements (microphone, resolution, confidence monitors, live coding terminals).
   - Add quotes or endorsements from conference organizers and program chairs.
   - Link your speaker press kit PDF, video reel, and booking email in the footer.

## Event Organizer Appeal

- Provides conference committees and organizers with all the information they need to review your CFP or book you as an invited keynote speaker.
