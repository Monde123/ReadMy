# Customization Guide: Magazine Editorial

The `013-magazine-editorial` template treats your developer profile as a high-density, thought-provoking technical periodical or engineering review.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Editorial Masthead**:
   - Customize publication name (e.g. `THE FRONTEND DISPATCH`, `THE DISTRIBUTED GAZETTE`).
   - Replace `Adrian Monroe` with your name.
   - Adjust Issue Number, City/Dateline, and circulation note.

2. **Lead Story & Issue Colophon**:
   - **Left Column**: Write a personal essay or engineering manifesto explaining what you care about in software design. Include a pull quote.
   - **Right Column**: Customize the Issue Index (table of contents), primary medium/stack, and current editorial status.

3. **Featured Case Studies & Selected Essays**:
   - Structure your projects as investigative Case Studies (with reading time, problem statement, and quantified outcomes).
   - List your technical blog posts, articles, or talks in the Selected Essays table.
   - Update the Correspondence & Subscription links in the footer.

## Typography & Theme Considerations

- Works naturally on both light and dark GitHub themes, emphasizing typographic hierarchy, horizontal rules, and balanced multi-column tables.
