# Customizing Magazine Editorial

This template is best for someone whose work blends storytelling, product work, and information design.

Replace the fictional magazine sections with your own themes, feature areas, and writing samples. You can keep the article-style layout even if your profile is more technical than editorial in tone.

If you include images, prefer neutral illustrations or editorial-style visuals that support the narrative rather than distract from it.

This layout is especially effective when your profile should feel polished, narrative-driven, and high-context.
