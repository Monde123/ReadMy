# Customizing Full-Stack & AI Engineer

This version of the template is intentionally product-facing: it reads more like a branded landing page than a standard engineering profile.

Replace the fictional profile, stack, projects, and links. Keep the flagship project grounded in actual system behavior, trade-offs, and boundaries rather than invented metrics or client claims.

The navigation is optional. Keep it when the README has enough sections to benefit from internal anchors and quick scanning.

This layout works well for product-engineering profiles, AI builders, and people who want to communicate technical depth without losing a strong sense of product clarity.
