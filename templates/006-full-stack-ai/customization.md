# Customization Guide: Full-Stack AI Engineer

The `006-full-stack-ai` template structures your profile around the complete end-to-end AI application lifecycle—from frontend streaming interfaces down to inference engines and telemetry.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Badges**:
   - Replace `Marcus Chen` with your name.
   - Adjust top badges to showcase your preferred frontend, backend orchestration, and vector storage stack.

2. **System Topology & Capability Matrix**:
   - Customize the ASCII system topology diagram with your actual architecture (e.g. streaming protocols, queue brokers, failover model providers).
   - Update the 4-tier capability table (Frontend, Orchestration, Memory, Evaluation).

3. **Flagship Products & Principles**:
   - Highlight 2 production full-stack AI products with architecture details, user adoption, and tech tags.
   - Update your personal principles on AI reliability and cost control.

## Design Advantages

- Clearly demonstrates that you can ship complete, production-grade applications rather than just isolated Jupyter notebooks.
- Clean GFM formatting guarantees fast loading and high contrast on all devices.
