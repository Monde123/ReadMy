# Customizing Full-Stack & AI Engineer

This is the most detailed template in the first collection. It is intended for a
builder who wants to show product thinking alongside implementation depth.

Replace the fictional profile, stack, projects, and links. Keep the flagship
project honest: describe system behavior, trade-offs, and boundaries rather than
inventing client names, user counts, or performance claims.

The navigation is optional. Keep it when the README has enough sections to benefit
from internal links.

Charts, banners, typing animations, and statistics may be added later, but they
should remain decorative or supporting and must use documented placeholders.
