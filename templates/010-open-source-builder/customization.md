# Customization Guide: Open Source Builder

The `010-open-source-builder` template provides an authoritative **Open Source Maintainer & Ecosystem Health Station**, tailored for library authors, toolmakers, and community stewards.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Maintainer Masthead & Stats**:
   - Replace `Mateo Silva` with your name and ecosystem focus.
   - Update top badges with your real or projected GitHub stars, monthly package downloads, PR triage turnaround time, and sponsor count.

2. **Repository Health Matrix & Flagship Tools**:
   - List your maintained packages with CI status badges, weekly download metrics, and links to open beginner issues.
   - Detail 2 flagship tools with quick-installation one-liners (`cargo add`, `npx`, `pip install`).

3. **Covenant & Sponsorship Tiers**:
   - Customize your open-source maintenance covenant and contribution philosophy.
   - Update the 3-tier sponsorship grid (Individual, Backer, Corporate) with your actual funding tiers and sponsor links.

## Ecosystem Impact

- Transforms your profile from a passive developer page into a welcoming, reliable open-source foundation that inspires both contributors and corporate sponsors.
