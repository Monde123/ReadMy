# Customizing Neon Command Center

Use this template when your work is high-velocity, systems-oriented, and best expressed as operational dashboards or mission panels.

Replace the fictional status panels with your own practice areas, systems, and active priorities. You can keep the command-center visual language even if your real work is not deeply technical.

If you use icons, dashboard art, or neon-themed visuals, keep them neutral and accessible. Do not add dense animation or obtrusive effects that reduce readability.
