# Customization Guide: Neon Command Center

The `014-neon-command-center` template turns your GitHub profile into a high-density, cyberpunk mission control cockpit tailored for SREs, DevOps engineers, and systems architects.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Station Header & Telemetry Badges**:
   - Replace `KAIZEN VEX` with your operator handle or name.
   - Adjust the 3 large `for-the-badge` status pills at the top (`System Status`, `Threat Level`, `Uptime`).
   - Define your Mission Directive statement inside the `[!IMPORTANT]` alert box.

2. **Telemetry Matrix & Active Operations**:
   - Update the 4 telemetry readout cells (e.g. Nodes, Throughput, Security status, p99 Latency).
   - Format your top 2 projects as tactical operations (`OPERATION [NAME]`) with payload specifications and impact telemetry.

3. **Tooling Arsenal & Comm Channels**:
   - Populate the Tactical Sector table with your platforms, languages, data stores, and observability tools.
   - Customize the ASCII communication box at the bottom with your GitHub handle, website, and ops email.

## Color Scheme & Visual Vibe

- Uses high-contrast neon badges (neon green `#00ff66`, cyan `#00f0ff`, hot magenta `#ff0055`) paired with pure monospace block borders.
- Renders powerfully against GitHub's dark theme while remaining 100% legible in light mode thanks to GFM fallback styles.
