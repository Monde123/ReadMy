# Customization Guide: Pitch Deck

The `024-pitch-deck` template formats your GitHub profile as an authentic **Venture Capital Pitch Deck & Technical Founder Profile**, built for startup founders, venture-backed engineers, and entrepreneurs raising capital.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Station Masthead & Traction Badges**:
   - Customize company name (`Synapse Cloud Labs`), your name, and prior exit/company pedigree.
   - Adjust top badges (Funding Round, ARR / Growth rate, Customer Count, Accelerator/Backers).

2. **The 5-Slide Structure**:
   - **Slide 01 (Pain Point)**: Frame the painful developer/enterprise friction and quantitative cost waste.
   - **Slide 02 (Solution)**: Present your technical architecture and performance breakthrough.
   - **Slide 03 (Traction Matrix)**: Populate the 4 key business KPIs (ARR, NRR, Active Orgs, Gross Margin).
   - **Slide 04 (Moat & Tech IP)**: Explain your patents, kernel IP, or network effects.
   - **Slide 05 (Team & Round Ask)**: Highlight founder backgrounds and current fundraising targets.

3. **Data Room & Inquiries**:
   - Link your DocSend pitch deck, founder scheduling calendar, and investor email.

## Founder Credibility

- Communicates both high-level venture economics and deep technical architecture in a clean GFM slide table.
- Ideal for showing technical leadership to prospective VCs, strategic angels, and senior engineering hires.
