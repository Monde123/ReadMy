# Customizing Timeline Journey

Use this template when your work is best explained through progress, milestones, and growth over time.

Replace the year headings, achievements, and focus areas with your own story. Keep the timeline order, or restructure it to reflect your actual path.

Avoid using real employment data unless it is verified. For your own profile, keep the narrative honest and concise.

This template works especially well for people whose value comes from iteration, long-term learning, and visible development across multiple stages.
