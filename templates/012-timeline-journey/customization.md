# Customization Guide: Timeline Journey

The `012-timeline-journey` template structures your developer profile as a **Narrative Engineering Timeline & Career Evolution**, perfect for seasoned engineers, architects, and technical leaders who want to share the hard-won wisdom behind their career.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Header & Career Metrics**:
   - Replace `Maya Lin` with your name.
   - Customize your career span (e.g., `2018 ➔ Present`), focus area, number of post-mortems published, and engineers mentored.

2. **Chronological Milestone Tree**:
   - Update the monospace year-by-year timeline with your actual roles, companies, notable deliverables, and real lessons learned.
   - Use the `[Lesson]` sub-bullets to demonstrate deep engineering reflection.

3. **Architectural Bets & Tech Lifecycle**:
   - Populate the Critical Architectural Bets table with technical decisions you championed, comparing the conventional consensus against the real-world outcome.
   - Organize your stack into Adopted, Maintained, and Retired technologies.

## Narrative Authenticity

- Builds instant respect and trust with senior technical recruiters and engineering directors.
- Demonstrates maturity, humility, and genuine technical depth rather than a static list of buzzwords.
