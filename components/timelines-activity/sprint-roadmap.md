## Customization Guide

- Replace quarter dates, goals, and deliverables.
- Status values can be: `SHIPPED` ✅, `IN PROGRESS` 🔄, `PLANNED` 📋, `EXPLORING` 🔬.
- Gives prospective employers and collaborators visibility into what you are actively building.
